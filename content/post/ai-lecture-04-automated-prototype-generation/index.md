## Abstract

The talk presents RM2PT, an approach and tool for automatically generating software prototypes from formal requirements models for requirements validation.

## Speaker biography

Dr. Yang earned his PhD in Software Engineering from the University of Macau in 2019 and was a Fellow at UNU-IIST. His research focuses on automated and intelligent software engineering.
