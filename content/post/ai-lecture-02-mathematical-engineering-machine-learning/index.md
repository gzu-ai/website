- Venue: Room 701, Boxue Building, Guizhou University

## Abstract

The talk reviews key stages in the development of machine learning, discusses four foundational principles, and proposes understanding machine learning as mathematical engineering.

## Speaker biography

Prof. Zhang works at the School of Mathematical Sciences and the Beijing Institute of Big Data Research, Peking University. His research focuses on statistical machine learning and artificial intelligence.
