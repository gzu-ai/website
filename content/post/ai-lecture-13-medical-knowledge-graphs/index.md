## Abstract

The lecture introduces medical knowledge graphs, their development and key technologies, and presents applications involving depression and gut microbiome knowledge graphs.

## Speaker biography

Prof. Huang works in the Artificial Intelligence department at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and studies semantic technologies, medical AI, and knowledge graphs.
