## Abstract

The lecture covers trustworthy AI models and systems, including transparency, explainability, fairness, robustness, trust, and reliability, with relevant case studies.

## Speaker biography

Prof. Liu is Professor of Computer Science and Director of the AI Research Centre at Ulster University. His work spans computational intelligence, trustworthy AI, knowledge systems, and information fusion.
