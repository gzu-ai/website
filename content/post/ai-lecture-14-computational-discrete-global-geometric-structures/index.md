## Abstract

The lecture introduces computational discrete global geometric structures, including geometric programming, global intrinsic properties in discrete meshes, visualization, and applications of heuristic algorithms.

## Speaker biography

Dr. Zhao has conducted research and teaching at universities and research institutes in China and abroad, focusing on computational discrete global geometric structures.
