## Abstract

The talk applies Approximation Fixpoint Theory to hybrid MKNF knowledge bases and discusses a generalization from symmetric to arbitrary approximators.

## Speaker biography

Prof. You is a professor at the University of Alberta. His research interests include artificial intelligence, logic programming, and knowledge representation and reasoning.
