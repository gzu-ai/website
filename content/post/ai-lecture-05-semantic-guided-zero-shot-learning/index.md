## Abstract

Zero-shot learning transfers semantic knowledge from seen to unseen classes. This talk presents a series of studies on effective visual-semantic interaction for zero-shot learning.

## Speaker biography

Dr. Chen studies computer vision and machine learning, including zero-shot learning, generative modeling, vision-language learning, and causal representation learning.
